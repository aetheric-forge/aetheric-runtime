# Constitutional Article — University

## Article X — University

### §1. Definition

A **University** is the unique root Institution of an institutional hierarchy.

Every institutional hierarchy **SHALL** contain exactly one University, from which all other Institutions derive their structural context.

The University establishes the identity, continuity, and constitutional boundary of the hierarchy.

---

### §2. Purpose

The University provides the common institutional context within which all subordinate Institutions exist.

It serves as the constitutional anchor for governance, identity, membership, and organizational relationships across every Campus it contains.

---

### §3. Root Authority

The University possesses no parent Institution.

It represents the highest level of structural authority within its own hierarchy.

No Institution may exist outside the University that defines its context.

---

### §4. Campuses

A University **MAY** contain one or more Campuses (Article VI).

Each Campus constitutes the root of its own descendant hierarchy, within the larger hierarchy rooted at its University.

The University **SHALL NOT** contain Institutions of any other kind directly, other than the Institutions it maintains under §6.

---

### §5. Recursive Hierarchy

Every Institution other than the University **SHALL** exist as a descendant of exactly one parent Institution.

This recursive relationship forms a rooted, directed, acyclic hierarchy.

The University constitutes the root of this hierarchy.

---

### §6. Institutional Capabilities

Every University **SHALL** maintain a Registrar.

The University's Registrar serves as the authoritative, shared source of identity, roles, relationships, and credentials for every Campus the University contains, so that Campuses need not each maintain a separate one.

The requirement that a University maintain a Registrar **SHALL NOT** make it an intrinsic property of every Institution.

---

### §7. Containment

Institutional containment represents constitutional ownership and administrative responsibility.

Containment **SHALL NOT** be inferred from communication, collaboration, governance, or operational interaction.

Only explicit parent-child relationships define structural containment.

---

### §8. Institutional Identity

The University defines the shared institutional identity of all descendant Institutions.

Individual Institutions, including each Campus, **MAY** possess their own identities, missions, and governance, but these **SHALL** exist within the constitutional context established by the University.

---

### §9. Continuity

The existence of subordinate Institutions, including every Campus, depends upon the constitutional continuity of the University.

Changes to the University **SHALL** preserve the continuity and integrity of the institutional hierarchy.

---

### §10. Technology Independence

The constitutional definition of a University is independent of any implementation technology, software architecture, database model, or deployment environment.

Implementations **SHALL** preserve the constitutional properties defined herein.

---

### §11. Invariants

Every University **SHALL** preserve the following invariants:

- Every institutional hierarchy contains exactly one University.
- The University has no parent Institution.
- Every other Institution is a descendant of the University.
- A University **MAY** contain one or more Campuses.
- Institutional containment forms a rooted, acyclic hierarchy.
- Structural containment **SHALL NOT** be inferred from communication or collaboration.
- The University defines the constitutional context of the hierarchy.
- The University maintains a Registrar, shared across every Campus it contains.
