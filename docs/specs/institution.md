# Constitutional Article — Institution

## Article III — Institution

### §1. Definition

An **Institution** is a persistent social construct established to pursue one or more enduring purposes through governance, organization, policy, and the coordinated stewardship of people, resources, and knowledge.

Within Aetheric, an Institution is represented as a declarative constitutional entity rather than an implementation. It describes **what exists** and **what is permitted**, while leaving **how it is executed** to the runtime.

---

### §2. Purpose

Every Institution **SHALL** possess one or more explicit purposes.

Purpose precedes structure.

The existence, governance, organization, capabilities, policies, and resources of an Institution derive from its declared purpose.

---

### §3. Identity

An Institution **SHALL** possess a stable identity independent of its current members, officers, assets, or operational state.

Its identity persists through changes in leadership, organization, policy, constitutional amendment, and implementation.

---

### §4. Governance

Every Institution **SHALL** define a system of governance.

Governance establishes:

- Authority
- Delegation
- Accountability
- Amendment
- Succession
- Dispute Resolution

Governance defines legitimate decision-making but does not itself execute those decisions.

---

### §5. Organization

An Institution organizes work through one or more Organizations.

Organizations provide operational structure.

An Organization **MAY** exist independently, but when operating within an Institution, derives its authority from that Institution's constitutional framework.

---

### §6. Policy

Institutions establish Policy.

Policy defines the constraints, permissions, obligations, and expectations governing behavior within the Institution.

Policies describe normative rules rather than implementation.

---

### §7. Capability

Institutions declare the capabilities necessary to fulfill their purposes.

Capabilities describe **what** may be performed.

The runtime determines **how** capabilities are realized.

---

### §8. Resources

Institutions steward resources.

Resources may include:

- People
- Knowledge
- Information
- Assets
- Services
- Infrastructure
- Financial Capital
- Time

Stewardship implies responsibility rather than ownership.

---

### §9. Composition

Institutions are compositional.

An Institution is defined by the composition of constitutional declarations including, but not limited to:

- Purpose
- Governance
- Organizations
- Roles
- Policies
- Capabilities
- Resources
- Workflows
- Events
- Constitutional Amendments

Composition **SHALL** be declarative.

---

### §10. Runtime

The Runtime **SHALL** interpret an Institution.

The Institution **SHALL NOT** prescribe execution mechanisms.

Execution, scheduling, persistence, messaging, authorization, orchestration, and lifecycle management are responsibilities of the Runtime.

---

### §11. Evolution

Institutions evolve through constitutional amendment.

Amendments **SHALL** preserve institutional identity while permitting changes to governance, organization, policy, capability, and operational structure.

Historical constitutional state **SHOULD** remain discoverable.

---

### §12. Invariants

Every Institution **SHALL**:

- Possess a stable identity.
- Declare one or more enduring purposes.
- Define governance.
- Organize authority.
- Steward resources.
- Express policy.
- Compose capabilities declaratively.
- Remain independent of runtime implementation.

No Institution **SHALL** depend upon a specific implementation technology to define its constitutional identity.
