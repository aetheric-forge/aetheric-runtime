# Article II — Knowledge

## Definition

Knowledge is information recognized by the Runtime as possessing meaning independent of its storage, transmission, or representation.

## Principles

- Knowledge exists independently of implementation.
- Knowledge may be created, referenced, communicated, preserved, and derived.
- Knowledge is represented through one or more immutable Knowledge Objects.
- The meaning of Knowledge SHALL remain distinct from the mechanisms by which it is stored or exchanged.

## Relationships

Knowledge may:

- be authored by an Identity;
- be stewarded by an Institution;
- be represented by Knowledge Objects;
- derive from other Knowledge;
- participate in a defined Lifecycle.

## Canons

- Knowledge SHALL be represented by immutable Knowledge Objects.
- A modification to Knowledge SHALL result in a distinct Knowledge Object.
- Institutions SHALL govern Knowledge without altering its immutable representations.
