# Article III — Institution

## Definition

An Institution is an organized authority within the Runtime established to govern a defined domain of responsibility according to declared principles and rules.

## Principles

- Institutions exist to govern, not merely to implement.
- Every Institution possesses a defined purpose.
- Institutions exercise authority only within their declared domain.
- Institutions interact through the common language established by this Ontology.

## Relationships

An Institution may:

- recognize Identities;
- steward Knowledge;
- grant or enforce Permissions;
- establish Policies;
- communicate with other Institutions.

## Canons

- An Institution SHALL govern a clearly defined domain.
- Institutions SHALL communicate through the concepts established by this Ontology.
- No Institution SHALL redefine the meaning of concepts established by this Constitution.
