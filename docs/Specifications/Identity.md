# Article I — Identity

## Definition

Identity is the persistent representation of an Entity within the Runtime. It establishes continuity of existence independent of authentication, authorization, or implementation.

## Principles

- Identity is persistent.
- Identity is unique within its governing context.
- Identity exists independently of any means by which it is verified.
- Identity may represent any Entity capable of participating within the Runtime.

## Relationships

An Identity may:

- possess Claims;
- be authenticated through one or more Schemes;
- be granted Permissions;
- belong to one or more Institutions;
- author or steward Knowledge.

## Canons

- Identity SHALL NOT be defined by its credentials.
- Authentication proves an Identity; it does not create one.
- Authorization governs the actions of an Identity; it does not define one.
