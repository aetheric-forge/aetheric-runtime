# Constitutional Article — Library

## Article V — Library

### §1. Definition

A **Library** is an Institution that collects, organizes, preserves, and makes knowledge available for discovery and use.

The Library provides a curated body of works, references, and resources in service of learning, inquiry, and institutional understanding.

The Library is not the constitutional record of the Institution.

---

### §2. Purpose

Every Campus **SHALL** maintain a Library.

The purpose of the Library is to make knowledge discoverable, intelligible, and reusable.

The Library exists to support education, research, practice, interpretation, and the continuing development of institutional knowledge.

---

### §3. Collection

The Library **MAY** collect works originating within or beyond the Institution.

Inclusion in the Library establishes that a work has been selected for institutional use or reference. It does not establish constitutional authority, historical truth, institutional endorsement, or exclusive validity.

The Library **SHALL** preserve the distinction between a work and the Institution's judgment concerning that work.

---

### §4. Curation

The Library **SHALL** curate its collection according to its purpose, governance, and policies.

Curation **MAY** include selection, classification, description, annotation, recommendation, revision, and withdrawal.

Curatorial authority does not confer authority to misrepresent a work, conceal its provenance, or alter its meaning without disclosure.

---

### §5. Organization and Discovery

The Library **SHALL** organize its collection sufficiently to support meaningful discovery.

Classification, indexing, cataloguing, and description **SHOULD** communicate the nature, origin, relationships, and intended use of collected works.

The organization of the Library **MAY** evolve as knowledge, language, and institutional needs change.

---

### §6. Provenance

Every collected work **SHALL** retain sufficient provenance to establish, where applicable:

- Origin
- Authorship
- Authority
- Edition or Version
- Time
- Context
- Terms of Use

The provenance of a work is part of its library record.

Uncertainty or dispute concerning provenance **SHALL** be represented honestly.

---

### §7. Accessibility

Library resources **SHOULD** remain discoverable and accessible to those entitled to use them.

Accessibility does not imply unrestricted visibility, possession, reproduction, or redistribution.

The Library **MAY** restrict access according to law, licence, policy, privacy, security, preservation requirements, or constitutional authority.

Restrictions **SHOULD** be no broader than required by their lawful purpose.

---

### §8. Plurality and Inquiry

The Library **SHOULD** support inquiry by preserving access to differing interpretations, methods, and schools of thought relevant to its purpose.

The presence of disagreement within the collection does not diminish the integrity of the Library.

The Library **SHALL NOT** present curatorial preference as historical fact or constitutional authority.

---

### §9. Revision and Withdrawal

Library records, classifications, annotations, and recommendations **MAY** be revised as knowledge and institutional needs evolve.

A work **MAY** be superseded, restricted, deprecated, or withdrawn from the active collection according to institutional policy.

Revision or withdrawal from the Library does not authorize destruction or alteration of the constitutional record maintained by the Archive.

---

### §10. Stewardship

The Library is stewarded on behalf of its Campus and the communities it serves.

Librarians safeguard the coherence, accessibility, provenance, and lawful use of the collection.

Administrative custody does not imply ownership of the knowledge represented by collected works.

---

### §11. Constitutional Relationship

The Library serves Persons, Communities, Organizations, Roles, and Institutions engaged in learning, inquiry, governance, and practice.

The Registrar establishes authoritative institutional facts.

The Archive preserves the historical and constitutional record.

The Library curates knowledge so that it may be discovered, understood, and used.

No act of collection, classification, annotation, recommendation, or withdrawal by the Library **SHALL**, by itself, amend the Register or the Archive.

---

### §12. Invariants

Every Library **SHALL**:

- Curate knowledge in service of learning and inquiry.
- Support meaningful discovery.
- Preserve the provenance of collected works.
- Distinguish curation from constitutional authority.
- Represent uncertainty and disagreement honestly.
- Respect lawful access and use restrictions.
- Remain independent of implementation technology.

No Library **SHALL** silently misrepresent the origin, authorship, authority, or meaning of a collected work.
